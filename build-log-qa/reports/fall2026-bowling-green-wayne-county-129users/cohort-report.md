**Rows #41–#54 were added by hand** during a recheck of the whole report against the conversations and a gap scan of the data (2026-10-27). They are log issues the audit doesn't raise as findings of their own:
- **#41:** the audit's coverage check asks only whether a student has *any* `argumentationToolEvent`, not whether each argumentation has one. Panel records from other units therefore hide the Unit 4 gap. The baseline playthrough has the same gap: its panel records come only from Units 1, 2, 3 and 5.
- **#42:** found by comparing each quest's starts with its finishes. The audit flags finishes without starts, not starts without finishes.
- **#43–#44:** the causes behind rows #2 and #33, given rows of their own because they are gaps.
- **#45:** found by comparing each argumentation's event types with the other argumentations.
- **#46–#48:** data-quality and delivery issues. They appear in sections 3, 5 and 6 as numbers but had no row here.
- **#49–#54:** value, naming and format issues found while reviewing the warnings.

The gap scan also compared, for each unit, every event type, action, conversation and quest in the baseline with the students who finished that unit. Nothing else is systematically missing: the remaining shortfalls are optional features (panels, map legend, Sprint, window switching) or rows above.

**Audit specification out of date.** These aren't log problems, but they produce or hide warnings: `event_expectations.yaml` lists the Soil Key Puzzle for Units 3–4 only, although over half its records come from Unit 2. ## 7. Method

- **D - too little data:** fewer than 60 active minutes.
- **C - partly usable:** ≥10% of the student's records are legacy-envelope records uploaded more than 5 s late (their `data` payload is a stale copy, so payload-based measures are unreliable for those periods; eventKey, eventType, sceneName and timestamps stay usable), or ≥5% of envelope sequence numbers missing.
- **B - usable with handling:** 1–10% stale-risk records, any smaller transport loss, a WASM runtime crash, or a FAIL finding.
- **A - ready:** none of the above.
- Not used for the verdict: findings that recur in the baseline playthrough (build-level quirks such as the DialogueEvent `…:0` first-node eventKey or the fixed-node eventKey/payload pairs), regression-category differences (pacing), absent events in units the student did not finish, and pointer-lock `unhandled_rejection` crash telemetry.
- **Logging design:** `current` = the log contains at least one of InputEvent, ObjectInterEvent, gameStartEvent, argumentationAnswerEvent; `reduced (older build)` = none of them (the April 2026 builds log 13 event types).
- Late uploads are serverTimestamp minus client timestamp. On the 20260914- build, 95% of student-days have a median difference within ±2 s, so larger values are upload delay, not clock error.
- Envelope = the `session_id`/`seq`/`entry_id`/`sent_at` fields present from build 20261005-12466; seq starts at 1 per session, so a missing number is a lost record and a repeated `entry_id` is a resend.
