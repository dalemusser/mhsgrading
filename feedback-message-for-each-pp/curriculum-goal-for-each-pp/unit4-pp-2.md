# Curriculum goal
At this progress point, players enter the alien ruins after completing the soil-key puzzle and encounter a glyph puzzle focused on groundwater movement through different subsurface materials.

The puzzle contains four pieces representing gravel, sand, clay, and a water-table element representing groundwater-saturated material. The corresponding slots represent different levels or rates of water movement. Players must interpret the characteristics represented by each piece and match it with the appropriate water-movement category.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ understanding that different subsurface materials allow water to move through them at different rates. Players learn that properties such as particle size and the spaces between particles influence how easily water can move through the ground. In general, water moves more readily through highly permeable materials such as gravel and more slowly through less permeable materials such as clay, with sand typically representing an intermediate condition. The water-table element reinforces the idea of a saturated subsurface zone in which pore spaces are filled with groundwater.

By completing the puzzle, players practice connecting different subsurface materials and groundwater conditions with differences in how water moves below the land surface.
