# Curriculum goal
After completing the quests in the desert zone, players travel to a new island environment and initially arrive inside an alien dungeon. To gain access to the next area of the dungeon, players must solve a glyph puzzle focused on evaporation.

The puzzle contains multiple glyph pieces representing different rates or levels of water evaporation, while the corresponding slots represent different environmental temperatures. Players must interpret the visual information shown on each glyph and match each evaporation representation with the appropriate temperature condition.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ understanding of the relationship between temperature and evaporation. Players learn that evaporation is the process by which liquid water changes into water vapor and enters the surrounding air. When other environmental conditions are similar, higher temperatures provide water molecules with more energy and generally increase the rate of evaporation, while lower temperatures generally result in slower evaporation.

By correctly matching the evaporation representations with different temperature conditions, players practice recognizing and applying the relationship between environmental temperature and the rate at which liquid water evaporates.
