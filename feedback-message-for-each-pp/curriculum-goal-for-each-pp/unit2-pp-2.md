# Curriculum goal
At this progress point, players first collect the necessary components and assemble a hoverboard, which allows them to travel more efficiently through the game environment. They then use clues gathered from previous conversations to determine Dr. Toppo’s likely location.

Players must interpret the topographic map, connect the conversational clues to specific geographic features or locations, and place a waypoint on the map to mark their prediction. They then navigate to the selected waypoint and verify whether Dr. Toppo is located there.

The curriculum goal of this progress point is to help players apply their topographic-map knowledge in a practical navigation task. More specifically, players learn to integrate map information with contextual clues, identify a target location, use a waypoint to plan their route, and navigate through the environment to test their conclusion.
