# Curriculum goal
At this progress point, players enter the dungeon containing the large Life Tree and must solve a glyph puzzle to unlock the door.

The puzzle contains three glyph pieces and three corresponding slots. Each piece presents a visual representation of a particular stage in the process of a soluble material dissolving in water. Players must interpret the scientific information shown on each piece and match it with the slot that represents the corresponding stage or complementary part of the dissolution process.

When a piece is placed in the correct slot, the piece and slot combine to form a complete visual representation of that stage, and the indicator above the image turns green to confirm the correct match.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ understanding of how soluble materials behave as they dissolve in water. Players practice interpreting scientific visual representations, distinguishing among different stages of the dissolution process, and matching related representations to form a scientifically coherent depiction of each stage.
