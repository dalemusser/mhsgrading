# Curriculum goal
At this progress point, players repair DANI, an AI companion in the game, by identifying the three core components of a scientific argument: claim, evidence, and reasoning.

Players are presented with a complete scientific argument containing highlighted passages. For each highlighted passage, they must determine whether it functions as a claim, evidence, or reasoning. The activity includes six classification decisions.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ ability to distinguish among the components of a scientific argument. Players learn that a claim states a conclusion or position, evidence provides relevant observations or information that support the claim, and reasoning explains how the evidence supports the claim. By examining each passage within the context of the whole argument, players practice identifying components according to their roles rather than relying only on isolated keywords.
