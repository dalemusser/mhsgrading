# Curriculum goal
This progress point extends the navigation task introduced in the previous progress point and provides players with additional opportunities to practice applying topographic-map skills.

Players must locate two other team members who are positioned in different areas of the game environment. To determine their locations, players gather relevant clues from conversations, connect those clues to geographic features shown on the topographic map, and use the map to identify and navigate toward each likely location.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ ability to integrate contextual clues with topographic-map information. Through repeated practice, players develop greater proficiency in identifying locations, planning routes, and using a topographic map to navigate through an unfamiliar environment.
