# Curriculum goal
After completing the third-floor puzzle, players continue solving more advanced soil and water-flow puzzles on Floors 4 and 5 of the alien-ruin basement. These puzzles increase in complexity by introducing multiple soil layers and multiple water pipes. Players must determine the appropriate combination and arrangement of subsurface materials so that water moves through the system at suitable levels and rates.

The first curriculum goal of this progress point extends the previous task by strengthening players’ ability to apply their understanding of soil permeability, water retention, and groundwater movement in more complex subsurface systems. Rather than selecting a single material, players must reason about how combinations of different materials influence water movement across multiple layers or pathways.

After successfully completing the basement puzzles, players obtain the complete drilling equipment. They must then determine the appropriate depth at which to drill in order to reach groundwater and bring water to the surface.

The second curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ understanding of where groundwater is located below the land surface. Players learn that groundwater occupies the saturated zone below the water table and that successfully accessing groundwater requires drilling to an appropriate depth that reaches this saturated zone.
