# Curriculum goal
At this progress point, players receive seeds from Tara and learn that several garden beds are located at different points along the river system. Players must use the topographic map and their knowledge of the identified pollution source to determine which garden beds are likely to be affected by the dissolved pollutant.

Players examine the location of the pollution source, the structure of the river network, and the direction of water flow. They then identify the garden beds located downstream of the pollution source and plant seeds in the beds that may be exposed to the contaminated water.

The curriculum goal is to strengthen players’ ability to apply their understanding of river flow and pollution transport to predict environmental impact. Players learn that dissolved pollutants can be carried downstream with flowing water, so locations connected to the river downstream of the pollution source are more likely to be affected, while locations upstream or along unconnected branches are less likely to be exposed.
