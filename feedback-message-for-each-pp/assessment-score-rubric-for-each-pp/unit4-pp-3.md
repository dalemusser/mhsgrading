# Alien Well: Third and Fourth Floors (Soil Machines)

This progress point combines the two soil-machine puzzles on the third and fourth floors of the alien well. For each floor, the score measures how many attempts the player needs to load the missing pipe section with the soil type that produces the required water flow.

## A. Third Floor (Room 3-2)

* **1 point:** The player selects **gravel** for the missing pipe section on the first attempt.
* **0 points:** Anything else (a different soil type first, more than one attempt, or the puzzle not attempted).

## B. Fourth Floor (Room 4-1)

* **2 points:** The player selects **sand** for the missing pipe section on the first attempt.
* **1 point:** The player selects **sand** on the second attempt.
* **0 points:** Anything else (more than two attempts, or the puzzle not attempted).

## Overall Score

The maximum combined score for this progress point is **3 points**.
