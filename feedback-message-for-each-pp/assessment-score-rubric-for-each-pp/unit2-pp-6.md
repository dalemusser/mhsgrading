# Select the Most Relevant Evidence

The score measures whether the player correctly identifies **water flow rate** as the most relevant observable factor for comparing the sizes of the Eastern and Western watersheds.

* **0.5 point:** The player selects **water flow rate**.
* **0 points:** The player selects any other factor, does not make a selection, or does not attempt the task.

The maximum score for this progress point is **0.5 point**.
