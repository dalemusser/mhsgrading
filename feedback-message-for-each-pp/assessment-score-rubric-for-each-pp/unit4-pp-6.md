# Tera’s Garden Boxes

The score measures whether the player places each recording camera on the garden box whose soil type matches the seedling’s water needs. There are three garden boxes, each with one correct soil type.

* **3 points:** The player places all 3 cameras on the correct soil.
* **2 points:** The player places 2 cameras on the correct soil.
* **1 point:** The player places 1 camera on the correct soil.
* **0 points:** No camera is placed on the correct soil, or the task is not attempted.

```text
Final Score = Number of correct camera placements (1 point each)
```

The maximum score for this progress point is **3 points**.
