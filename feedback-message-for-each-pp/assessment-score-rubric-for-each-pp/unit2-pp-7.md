# Argue Which Watershed Is Larger

The score measures how many attempts the player needs to submit a scientifically correct argument identifying whether the Eastern or Western watershed is larger.

## Scoring Criteria

* **3 points:** The player submits the correct argument within 1 to 3 attempts.
* **2 points:** The player submits the correct argument on the 4th attempt.
* **1 point:** The player submits the correct argument on the 5th attempt.
* **0 points:** The player requires more than 5 attempts, does not submit a correct argument, or does not attempt the task.

The maximum score for this progress point is **3 points**.

## Correct-Response Feedback

When the player submits the correct argument, Dr. Toppo provides the following feedback:

> Well done! You have made the best argument possible. Good job, Cadet!
