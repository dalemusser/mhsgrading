# Well Wishes and the Soil Key Puzzle

This progress point combines two items: Anderson’s question about the water table in the Well Wishes dialogue, and the Unit 4 soil key puzzle.

## A. Water-Table Question (Well Wishes dialogue)

The score measures whether the player selects the correct definition of the water table.

* **0.5 point:** The player selects “It’s the boundary between saturated and unsaturated soil layers.”
* **0 points:** The player selects any other answer or does not make a selection.

## B. Soil Key Puzzle (completion time)

The score measures how long the soil key puzzle window stays open, from the moment the puzzle starts until it is finished.

* **1 point:** The player completes the puzzle within 30 seconds.
* **0.5 point:** The player completes the puzzle in more than 30 seconds but within 90 seconds.
* **0 points:** The player takes more than 90 seconds, or does not complete the puzzle.

## Overall Score

The maximum combined score for this progress point is **1.5 points**.
