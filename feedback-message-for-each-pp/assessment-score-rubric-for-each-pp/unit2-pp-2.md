# Place the Waypoint at Dr. Toppo’s Location
The score measures whether the player places the waypoint at Dr. Toppo’s correct location on the topographic map.

* **1 point:** The player places the waypoint at Dr. Toppo’s correct location.
* **0 points:** The waypoint is placed at an incorrect location, no waypoint is placed, or the task is not attempted.

The maximum score for this progress point is **1 point**.
