# Classify Scientific Argument Components

Players classify six highlighted passages from a complete scientific argument as a **claim**, **evidence**, or **reasoning**. The activity is completed after all six passages have been classified correctly.

#### Scoring for Each Placement Attempt

* **Correct placement:** +1 point
* **Incorrect placement:** −0.33 points

#### Final Score Calculation

The final score is normalized by the total number of placement attempts:

```text
Final Score =
[(Number of Correct Placements × 1)
+ (Number of Incorrect Placements × −0.33)]
÷ Total Number of Placement Attempts
```

The total number of placement attempts is calculated as:

```text
Total Number of Placement Attempts =
Number of Correct Placements
+ Number of Incorrect Placements
```

Players must eventually make **six correct placements** to complete the activity. Incorrect attempts reduce the final score. Therefore, players who classify all six passages correctly with fewer errors receive higher scores.

#### Example

Suppose a player makes:

* **6 correct placements**
* **2 incorrect placements**
* **8 total placement attempts**

The score is calculated as follows:

```text
Final Score =
[(6 × 1) + (2 × −0.33)] ÷ 8

Final Score =
(6 − 0.66) ÷ 8

Final Score =
5.34 ÷ 8

Final Score =
0.6675
```

Therefore, the player’s final score is **0.6675**, which may be rounded to **0.67**.

#### Scoring Clarification

The final rubric should specify whether a calculated score below zero is:

* retained as a negative score; or
* adjusted to a minimum score of **0**.
