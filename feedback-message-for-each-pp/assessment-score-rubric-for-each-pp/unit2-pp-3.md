# Locate Tera and Aryn
This progress point contains two navigation tasks. For each task, the score measures both the accuracy of the player’s waypoint placement and the player’s use of the map or successful completion within the specified time limit.

## A. Place a Waypoint and Find Tera

**Waypoint-placement score**

* **1 point:** The player places the waypoint at Tera’s correct location.
* **0 points:** The player places the waypoint at an incorrect location, does not place a waypoint, or does not attempt the task.

**Navigation-performance score**

* **1 point:** The player finds Tera within five minutes.
* **0.5 point:** The player does not find Tera within five minutes but opens the topographic map during the task.
* **0 points:** The player neither finds Tera within five minutes nor opens the map, or does not attempt the task.

The maximum score for the Tera task is **2 points**.

## B. Place a Waypoint and Find Aryn

**Waypoint-placement score**

* **1 point:** The player places the waypoint at Aryn’s correct location.
* **0 points:** The player places the waypoint at an incorrect location, does not place a waypoint, or does not attempt the task.

**Navigation-performance score**

* **1 point:** The player finds Aryn within three minutes.
* **0.5 point:** The player does not find Aryn within three minutes but opens the topographic map during the task.
* **0 points:** The player neither finds Aryn within three minutes nor opens the map, or does not attempt the task.

The maximum score for the Aryn task is **2 points**.

## Overall Score

The maximum combined score for this progress point is **4 points**.
