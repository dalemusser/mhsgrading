**\[Cutscene\] Finding the Main Hall:**  
**\[Camera performs a sweeping pan around the main hall\]**

The camera sweeps down as Player, Tera, and Jasper enter the enormous 5-sided main room, 60 meters to each side. The vertical walls stretch up to a flat ceiling, also about 60 meters above the player. A chute emits from each of the other four walls (all except the entrance wall) about 2/3rds of the way to the ceiling. A large, off-center window is set high near the top of each wall. They allow diagonal slashes of natural white light down into the room, cutting dramatic light/dark chiaroscuro across the scene. 

They find themselves at the top of a small staircase that leads down several meters to the main floor (each wall has an identical set of stairs leading from a hexagonal door down to the central area). In the center of the room several meters below the players stands an ENORMOUS tree, easily 30 meters tall, completely ossified, brittle, like a solidified piece of ash rather than a tree. It appears to be the same species as the tree near home base, only many, many times more massive. A lone, large fruit– the size of a basketball–hangs heavily off of one low branch, its pitted surface like a green orange.

The tree is growing from a small, domed island just slightly larger than its trunk in the center of a large, deep reservoir of dark water that fills most of the room. The water slowly churns around the circular reservoir, and below each wall-chute, a small channel for water cuts down directly to this reservoir–the effect being that the chutes would seem to discharge liquid that feeds the central reservoir. One of the chutes is slowly discharging a thick, faintly luminescent green liquid into its chute and flowing down into the reservoir.

On one wall, below the chute, a six-segmented symbol is etched deeply into the wall. On another wall, at the Player’s height, is an ALIEN MURAL.
