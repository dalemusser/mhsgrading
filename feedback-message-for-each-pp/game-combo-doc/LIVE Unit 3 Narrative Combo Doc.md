The Player steps through the flap of their tiny tent and looks around appraisingly, as the camera then soars away to survey the camp.

Tents and construction debris litter the ground all around the player–the chaos that is an entire temporary shelter constructed over the last week. The semi-circle of four hexagonal white tents–the individual cadet quarters– are stuffed into the back (North) corner of the not-quite-perfect diamond shape that forms the current footprint of the base. Behind the tents, a dense thicket of what appear to be thick birch trees with yellow/gold hued leaves stand before the ground gives way to a rocky escarpment–perfect natural protection.

The sound of automated machinery–Anderson’s?--hums in the background in unobtrusive, arcane ways.

Ahead of the player, to the left (East), Toppo’s khaki-colored, Kodiak canvas tent stands with the tent flaps open. Beside it, the handful of plastic tables and chairs that form a rudimentary outdoor mess next to what appears to be a domed metal oven. A monitor is set up haphazardly on a post facing the chairs. 

To the right (West), blue tarps are strapped down tight over top of chunky, enormous, geometric shapes that must be the remaining equipment and supplies they have recovered–but not yet deployed. There must be enough there to construct at least several more buildings…behind them runs the river that flows North-South.

In the center of the space, a large, well-constructed stone firepit, at least 5 feet across, stands with several logs pulled up for seats. Though primitive, it feels warm, inviting, clearly a space where the cadets gather in the evening.

To the front (South), there is an open break in the low fence–essentially “cutting off the corner” of the diamond. Beyond the opening, the land gradually slopes away, dotted with lone shrubs and the occasional copse of trees.

**\[Cinematic: first person view from WAT247, looks up, sees light in sky, crash zooms to Copernicus in orbit\]**

First person view (arms and legs occasionally visible) from the eyes of a blue-light hologram, vaguely human in shape, translucent and shimmering, as it walks across the fields of WAT247 in the day, watching the biomechanical animals cavorting. It reaches down to scratch the chin of an alligator that wags its tail.

SUDDENLY, the alligator looks up behind itself, concerned, and a warning klaxon sounds a moment later. The first person view looks frantically in the sky, then spots a small light arcing across the sky.

With a CRASH ZOOM up through the atmosphere, the COPERNICUS is suddenly visible in orbit (still intact, as when it originally came to WAT247).
