# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. After completing the desert-zone quests, players are transported to a tropical-island environment and begin Unit 5 inside an underground alien dungeon. To proceed, they must solve a glyph puzzle focused on the relationship between **environmental temperature and the rate of water evaporation**.

2. Dr. Toppo provides instructional content introducing the relevant subject-matter knowledge for the unit. Players can use this tutorial to review evaporation as the process in which liquid water changes into water vapor and to understand that, under similar environmental conditions, higher temperatures generally lead to faster evaporation.

3. DANI provides dialogue and hints that reinforce the concepts introduced in Dr. Toppo’s tutorial and help players interpret what the different glyph pieces and temperature-related slots represent.

4. Players can carefully compare the visual representations shown on the glyph pieces with the temperature conditions represented by the available slots. They can use the relationship between temperature and evaporation rate to determine which pieces should be matched with which slots.

5. Players can compare the available pieces relative to one another rather than evaluating each piece independently. For example, a representation showing a greater degree or faster rate of evaporation should generally correspond to a higher-temperature condition, while a representation showing less or slower evaporation should correspond to a lower-temperature condition.

6. Each time players submit a set of matches, DANI provides feedback indicating whether the arrangement is correct. When the submission is incorrect, DANI provides additional hints to help players identify which relationship or matching decision may need revision.

7. DANI’s support can become more explicit when players continue to struggle. Depending on the number of unsuccessful attempts, the support may include restating key concepts, replaying Dr. Toppo’s instructional content, providing more direct hints, or helping players complete the remaining matches.

8. Players can use the combination of Dr. Toppo’s tutorial, DANI’s dialogue, the visual information in the glyph puzzle, and feedback from previous submissions to revise their matches until the evaporation representations are correctly paired with the corresponding temperature conditions.
