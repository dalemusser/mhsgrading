# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. Players can apply the topographic-map knowledge, clue-interpretation skills, and navigation strategies developed during the previous progress points. This activity extends the earlier navigation task by asking players to locate two additional team members in different areas of the game environment.

2. Players receive narrative clues about each team member’s location through conversations. These clues may describe directions, elevations, landforms, nearby water features, or other geographic characteristics that can be identified on the topographic map.

3. Players can identify important geographic details in the dialogue and connect them to corresponding features on the topographic map. Because the two team members are located in different places, players must interpret and apply each set of clues separately.

4. Players can place waypoints on the topographic map to mark the locations where they believe each team member can be found. They can then use the waypoints to plan their routes and navigate toward the selected locations.

5. The relevant location descriptions remain accessible while players use the topographic map, allowing them to compare the narrative clues directly with the map. Players can also open the chat tool to review previous conversations when they need to revisit or verify a clue.

6. While navigating, players can compare the terrain and geographic features they encounter with the clues provided in the conversations. This helps them evaluate whether they are traveling in an appropriate direction and approaching the intended location.

7. When players travel toward an incorrect location or continue along a route that is inconsistent with the narrative clues, reminder dialogues are triggered. These reminders explain why the current path is unlikely to lead to the team member and redirect the player’s attention to the relevant directions or geographic features.

8. Players can use these reminders to reconsider their interpretation of the clues, revise their selected waypoint or route, and continue searching until both team members are located.
