# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. Immediately before beginning the puzzle, players can watch an instructional video presented by Dr. Toppo. The video introduces key watershed concepts, including how water drains across the landscape toward a common river or outlet and how watershed size can relate to river flow rate.

2. Players can carefully examine and compare the terrain pieces, which represent watersheds with different landforms, elevations, slopes, and drainage-area sizes. They must identify the relevant characteristics of each piece and place it into the corresponding watershed or flow-rate category.

3. The slots are arranged to represent increasing relative watershed size or flow rate, with categories farther to the right representing greater values. Players can compare the terrain pieces and look for the pattern that a larger drainage area can collect and deliver more water to the main river, potentially producing a greater flow rate under similar environmental conditions.

4. Players can compare pieces with one another rather than judging each piece in isolation. Considering their relative size, shape, slope, and drainage configuration can help players determine the correct ordering and placement.

5. After submitting their matches, players receive information about whether all pieces were placed correctly. When the solution is incorrect, players can also learn how many pieces were mismatched, allowing them to reconsider only the placements that may need revision.

6. DANI provides adaptive feedback based on the number of attempts the player has made and the number of pieces placed incorrectly. Early feedback may restate key watershed concepts or direct attention to relevant terrain characteristics.

7. If players continue to experience difficulty, DANI may provide increasingly explicit support, such as offering targeted hints for incorrect placements, explaining the relationship represented by particular pieces, or helping players place the remaining pieces correctly.

8. Players can use DANI’s feedback to compare their current arrangement with the scientific concepts introduced in the video, revise incorrect matches, and gradually identify the pattern connecting drainage-area size with relative river flow rate.
