# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. Players can learn the basic concepts of scientific argumentation by watching an instructional video presented by Dr. Toppo.

2. Players can obtain additional information about claims, evidence, and reasoning through conversations with Dr. Toppo and DANI.

3. Players can review and strengthen their understanding of scientific argumentation by selecting optional dialogue choices that allow them to ask Dr. Toppo questions about the key concepts.

4. Players can learn how to open, navigate, and interact with the argumentation tool by following DANI’s step-by-step guidance. During this tutorial, DANI also reinforces the definitions and functions of claims, evidence, and reasoning.

5. Players can use the reference or help feature within the argumentation tool to review the key concepts whenever they need additional support.

6. Players can hover over available claim, evidence, and reasoning nodes to read the information contained in each option. Comparing these descriptions can help them determine how the components are related and identify the claim that is logically supported by the provided evidence and reasoning.

7. After submitting their response, players can use Dr. Toppo’s feedback to evaluate and revise their understanding. The feedback indicates whether the response is correct and, when it is incorrect, explains the specific error the player made.
