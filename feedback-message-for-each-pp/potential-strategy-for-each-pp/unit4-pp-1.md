# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. After arriving in the desert zone, players learn from DANI that Anderson needs their help. Before beginning the main task, DANI introduces Dr. Toppo’s instructional content about groundwater and the water table. Players can use this tutorial to build the subject-matter knowledge needed for the upcoming activities.

2. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to review previous conversations and instructional information. This allows them to revisit explanations about groundwater, the water table, and relevant task instructions if they need additional support.

3. Anderson asks the player a question about the meaning of the water table. Players can use the knowledge introduced in Dr. Toppo’s tutorial to compare the available dialogue choices and select the response that most accurately represents the concept.

4. After the player selects an answer to Anderson’s question, feedback is provided indicating whether the response is correct. If the answer is incorrect, the feedback helps clarify the concept and gives the player an opportunity to refine their understanding of the water table.

5. During the soil-key puzzle, players can apply what they have learned about groundwater and subsurface materials. They can compare the available puzzle elements, including clay, sand, gravel, and the water-table element, and observe how different choices affect the resulting groundwater level.

6. Players can use the visible target range in the puzzle to evaluate whether the current combination of subsurface elements produces an appropriate groundwater condition. If the resulting water level is too high or too low, they can revise their selections and test a different combination.

7. If the player remains inactive for an extended period or appears to be struggling with the puzzle, DANI can provide reminder dialogue or hints. These prompts direct the player’s attention to relevant groundwater or soil concepts and help them reconsider how the available puzzle elements influence the water level.

8. Players can use the combination of Dr. Toppo’s tutorial, Anderson’s feedback, the chat-history tool, visual feedback from the puzzle, and DANI’s hints to revise their understanding and successfully complete the task.
