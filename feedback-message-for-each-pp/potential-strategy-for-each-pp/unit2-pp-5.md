# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. To help repair DANI’s argumentation algorithm, players identify whether each highlighted passage in a complete scientific argument functions as a claim, evidence, or reasoning.

2. Players can apply the argumentation knowledge developed in earlier activities, including the definitions and roles of claims, evidence, and reasoning. A claim states the conclusion, evidence provides information that supports the claim, and reasoning explains how the evidence supports the claim.

3. Players can examine each highlighted passage within the context of the complete argument rather than judging it in isolation. They can consider whether the passage presents a conclusion, supplies supporting information, or explains the logical relationship between the evidence and the claim.

4. After each selection, Dr. Toppo provides immediate feedback indicating whether the classification is correct.

5. When a player makes an incorrect selection, Dr. Toppo explains the specific misunderstanding and clarifies the actual function of the highlighted passage. Players can use this feedback to refine their understanding and avoid making the same type of classification error in later selections.
