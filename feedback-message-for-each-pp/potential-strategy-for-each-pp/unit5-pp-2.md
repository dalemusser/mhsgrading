# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. After entering the multi-floor alien dungeon, players first complete puzzles on Floors 1 and 2 that introduce the mechanics of the **evaporation and condensation machines**. These practice floors help players understand how the two machines affect the phase and movement of water within the puzzle system.

2. Players can use the early floors to observe how different machine settings influence whether water evaporates into water vapor, condenses back into liquid water, and ultimately creates the conditions needed to move the key cube toward the lock station.

3. Beginning on Floor 3, the puzzles become more complex and require players to apply the knowledge and machine-operation strategies developed on the earlier floors with greater independence.

4. Players can draw on their previous successful configurations and compare them with the requirements of the current puzzle. Recognizing similarities and differences between floors can help them determine which evaporation and condensation settings are likely to produce the desired result.

5. Each time players interact with an evaporation or condensation machine, they can immediately observe the resulting changes in the water system and the movement of the key cube. These outcomes provide direct feedback about whether the current machine configuration is moving the system closer to or farther from the desired condition.

6. Players can use a process of **prediction, observation, and adjustment**. Before changing a machine, they can predict how the change will affect evaporation, condensation, and water movement; after observing the outcome, they can revise the configuration if necessary.

7. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to review previous conversations, instructions, and hints related to evaporation, condensation, and the dungeon puzzles.

8. Players can review Dr. Toppo’s instructional content to reinforce the relevant subject-matter knowledge, particularly that evaporation changes liquid water into water vapor, while condensation changes water vapor back into liquid water.

9. On the more complex later floors, players can combine prior puzzle-solving experience, scientific knowledge, observed machine outcomes, and information from previous conversations to determine an effective sequence or combination of evaporation and condensation settings.

10. Players can progressively refine their understanding by comparing successful and unsuccessful machine configurations, reducing unnecessary adjustments as they become more proficient in predicting how the two phase-change processes interact within the system.
