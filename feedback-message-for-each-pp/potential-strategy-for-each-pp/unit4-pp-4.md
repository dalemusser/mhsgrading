# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. Players can draw on their experience from the earlier dungeon floors when solving the more complex soil and water-flow puzzles on the later floors, including Floor 5. These earlier puzzles provide practice in predicting how different subsurface materials affect water movement, water level, and the movement of the key cube.

2. The later-floor puzzles increase in complexity by requiring players to reason about multiple soil layers, water pathways, or pipe systems at the same time. Players can compare the outcomes of different material combinations and use what they learned from earlier floors to determine which arrangement produces the required water conditions.

3. Players can review Dr. Toppo’s earlier instructional content and apply knowledge from previous groundwater-related quests, particularly concepts involving soil permeability, water retention, groundwater movement, and the location of the water table.

4. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to revisit previous conversations, instructional explanations, and feedback from earlier puzzle attempts when they need additional guidance.

5. During the soil-machine puzzles, DANI provides feedback and reminders that can help players evaluate whether their current material choices are producing the appropriate water conditions. Players can use this feedback to revise incorrect configurations and test alternative combinations.

6. Players can also use the immediate outcome of each machine configuration as feedback. If the water level, water flow, or key-cube movement does not produce the intended result, they can compare the outcome with previous attempts and adjust one or more soil selections accordingly.

7. After completing the dungeon puzzles and obtaining the full drilling equipment, players must determine the appropriate floor level or depth at which to drill for groundwater. They can apply their understanding that groundwater is located in the saturated zone below the water table.

8. Players can use information learned about the vertical location of groundwater, together with the environmental and subsurface information provided during the quest, to predict which drilling depth is most likely to reach the saturated zone.

9. After selecting a drilling location or depth, players receive feedback indicating whether the choice is correct. If the selection is incorrect, the feedback helps explain why that level does not successfully access groundwater and can guide the player toward a more appropriate depth.

10. Players can combine prior puzzle-solving experience, Dr. Toppo’s instruction, DANI’s feedback, chat-history information, and the observed outcomes of both soil configurations and drilling attempts to progressively solve the task.
