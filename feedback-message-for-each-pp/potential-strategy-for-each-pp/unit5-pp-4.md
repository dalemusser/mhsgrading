# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. Players are asked to design a water-generation system by applying the knowledge of evaporation and condensation developed throughout Unit 5. Conversations with Aryn, Dr. Toppo, and Anderson provide additional information about the key design features needed for a functional water-collection system.

2. Players can use their prior experiences from the evaporation and condensation puzzles, as well as the water-factory investigation, to reason about how water should move through the system and change between liquid water and water vapor.

3. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to review previous conversations and revisit information about evaporation, condensation, temperature, roof structure, and water collection.

4. The design task requires players to make several component-level decisions, including the **roof shape**, whether an **additional roof** should be included, and whether the roof surface should be **cold or hot**. Players can consider how each choice may affect condensation and the movement of collected water.

5. Players can review the structure or blueprint while making design selections. The blueprint allows them to evaluate how the selected components fit together and whether the proposed design is likely to operate as an effective water-generation and collection system.

6. Players can apply relevant scientific principles when evaluating the design. For example, a colder surface can promote condensation of water vapor into liquid water, while an appropriately sloped roof can help direct condensed water toward a collection location.

7. Players should evaluate the design as an **integrated system** rather than judging each component independently. A successful design depends on the selected components working together to support water-vapor condensation and effective collection of the resulting liquid water.

8. After submitting a design, Dr. Toppo provides feedback about the performance of the proposed system, including how much water it is expected to generate and whether it represents the most effective design.

9. If the submitted design is not optimal, Dr. Toppo’s feedback explains why one or more design choices reduce the effectiveness of the system. Players can use this information to reconsider the relationship between temperature, condensation, roof structure, and water collection.

10. Players can combine prior Unit 5 knowledge, NPC guidance, chat-history information, blueprint inspection, and Dr. Toppo’s design feedback to refine their understanding of how evaporation and condensation principles can be applied to an engineering solution for generating and collecting water.
