# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. After the dungeon puzzle is completed and the Life Tree is restored, players observe that a pipe near the dungeon begins releasing water containing dissolved Life Tree material into the surrounding river system. Tara then provides seeds and asks players to determine which garden beds are likely to receive this material, because exposure to it can support faster or stronger plant growth.

2. Players can apply knowledge developed in earlier Unit 3 quests about river flow, upstream and downstream relationships, dissolved-material transport, and river connectivity. These concepts provide the main foundation for predicting where the Life Tree material will travel after entering the river.

3. Players can use the topographic map to locate the pipe where the dissolved material enters the river, examine the connected river network, and identify the garden beds positioned along relevant downstream flow paths.

4. Players can place waypoints on the topographic map to mark garden beds they believe are likely to be affected. They can then use the waypoint as a navigation aid to travel efficiently to the selected planting location.

5. Players should consider not only whether a garden bed is geographically close to the river, but also whether it is located **downstream of and connected to the point where the Life Tree material enters the water**. Beds located upstream or along river branches that are not connected to the contaminated flow path are less likely to receive the dissolved material.

6. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to review previous conversations with Tara and other NPCs. These conversations may contain information about the task, river flow, the Life Tree material, or the locations that should be considered.

7. After players plant seeds in a garden bed, Tara provides feedback indicating whether the selected location is appropriate. When a selection is incorrect, her feedback can help players understand why that bed is unlikely to receive the Life Tree material.

8. Players can use Tara’s feedback to reconsider the river-flow pathway, revise their interpretation of which beds are downstream of the release point, and select more appropriate planting locations for the remaining seeds.

9. Players can combine information from previous quests, the topographic map, river-flow direction, waypoint placement, chat history, and Tara’s feedback to progressively determine which garden beds are most likely to be influenced by the dissolved Life Tree material.
