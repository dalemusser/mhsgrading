# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. Players can see the instructional video about topographic maps presented by Dr. Toppo during Unit 1. The video introduces foundational concepts that can help players complete the matching puzzle in this progress point.

2. DANI provides additional instruction through dialogue and an interactive tutorial using the topographic-map interface. This guidance explains important map components and functions, including cardinal directions, land and water features, contour lines, elevation, and the relationship between a top-down map view and the shape of the terrain.

3. Players can carefully examine the visual characteristics of each topographic map and compare them with the corresponding terrain or elevation-profile options. They can use features such as contour-line patterns, elevation changes, slopes, peaks, valleys, and the relative locations of land and water to identify the correct match.

4. When players make an incorrect match, DANI provides feedback and additional support. The type and level of support adapt according to the number of incorrect attempts and the player’s overall progress in the puzzle.

5. DANI’s adaptive support may include restating relevant topographic-map concepts, repeating previously provided hints, directing the player to review the instructional video, or explaining which visual features should be compared.

6. When a player continues to experience difficulty, DANI may provide more explicit assistance by helping match the remaining pieces and explaining why each topographic map corresponds to a particular terrain or elevation profile.
