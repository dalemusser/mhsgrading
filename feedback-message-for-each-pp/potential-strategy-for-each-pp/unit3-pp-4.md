# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. Players can gain relevant knowledge from conversations with Tara, Aryn, and Dr. Toppo about how soluble materials behave when they dissolve in water and how different stages of the dissolution process can be visually represented.

2. Players can use the in-game chat tool to review previous conversations when they need to revisit explanations, clues, or terminology related to dissolution.

3. Players can review Dr. Toppo’s earlier instructional content to reinforce the scientific concepts needed for the puzzle, particularly how a soluble material changes and becomes distributed in water during the dissolution process.

4. Players can apply knowledge developed through previous quests involving dissolved pollution in the river. These earlier activities provide contextual experience with the idea that a dissolved substance can become mixed with and transported by water.

5. Players can carefully examine the visual information shown on each glyph piece and compare it with the corresponding slot. They can look for how the particles or materials are represented and determine which piece and slot together form a scientifically coherent representation of a particular stage of dissolution.

6. Players can compare all available pieces and slots rather than evaluating each one independently. Looking for patterns across the complete set can help them distinguish among the different stages and identify the correct matches.

7. After submitting a set of matches, players receive feedback from DANI. When the submission is incorrect, DANI provides reminder dialogue or hints that help players identify what is wrong with the current arrangement.

8. Players can use DANI’s feedback together with the scientific information available from previous conversations, tutorials, and quests to reconsider incorrect matches and revise the puzzle until all three dissolution representations are correctly completed.
