# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. To unlock the elevator, players must solve a glyph puzzle focused on how water moves through different subsurface materials. Players can draw on knowledge developed in the previous progress point, including information about groundwater, the water table, and the properties of clay, sand, and gravel.

2. Players can review Dr. Toppo’s earlier instructional content and use the in-game chat-history tool to revisit relevant explanations from previous conversations. These resources can help reinforce the relationship between subsurface material properties and the ease or rate of water movement.

3. Players can apply knowledge gained from earlier quests and puzzles, particularly their observations of how different subsurface materials influence groundwater behavior.

4. Players can carefully inspect each glyph piece and compare it with the available water-movement categories. They can consider characteristics such as particle size, permeability, and how easily water can move through each material.

5. Players can compare the available pieces relative to one another rather than evaluating each one independently. For example, gravel generally allows water to move more readily than sand, while clay generally allows slower water movement. The water-table element should be interpreted according to the groundwater representation used within the game.

6. After players submit their matches, DANI provides feedback indicating whether the arrangement is correct.

7. When one or more matches are incorrect, DANI provides hints explaining why the current placement does not fit the represented water-movement condition. Players can use this feedback to reconsider the properties of the selected material and revise the placement.

8. Players can combine prior groundwater knowledge, the visual information shown in the glyphs, comparisons among the available materials, and DANI’s corrective feedback to progressively determine the correct set of matches and unlock the elevator.
