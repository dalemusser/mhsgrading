# Information and Strategies Available to Players
1. Players can apply the watershed knowledge introduced in earlier activities, particularly the relationship between drainage-area size and water flow rate under comparable environmental conditions.

2. During the drone investigation, players collect observations from the Western and Eastern Falls, including water flow rate, waterfall height, water salinity, and distance from the waterfall to the ocean.

3. Dialogues presented during the investigation provide explanations and hints about the meaning and relevance of the collected observations. Players can pay attention to these dialogues to determine which factor is most useful for comparing watershed size.

4. Players can use the in-game chat log to review previous conversations and revisit information provided during evidence collection. This allows them to recover missed details and summarize the important clues before making a decision.

5. After all required observations have been collected, a comparison table displays the evidence from both waterfalls side by side. Players can use the table to identify differences between the locations and evaluate which factor most directly relates to watershed size.

6. Players can compare the scientific relevance of the available factors rather than simply selecting the largest numerical difference. Within this activity, water flow rate is the most relevant indicator because a larger drainage area can collect and deliver more water to its outlet when other environmental conditions are comparable.

7. When players select a factor, Dr. Toppo provides immediate feedback indicating whether the choice is correct. If the selection is incorrect, the feedback explains why that factor—such as waterfall height, salinity, or distance from the ocean—does not directly indicate watershed size.

8. Players can use Dr. Toppo’s feedback to reconsider the evidence, distinguish relevant from irrelevant observations, and strengthen their understanding of why flow rate provides the best evidence within the context of the investigation.
