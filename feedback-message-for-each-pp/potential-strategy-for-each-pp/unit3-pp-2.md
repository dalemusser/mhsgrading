# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. Tara and DANI provide instructions about the pollution sensors, including what the sensors detect, how to deploy them, and how to interpret the results.

2. Players can apply previously learned knowledge about water flow, river connectivity, and upstream/downstream relationships from Dr. Toppo’s instruction and the earlier crate-delivery quest. This knowledge provides an important foundation for tracing the pollution source through the river network.

3. Each time a player deploys a sensor into the river, DANI reports the result. A **red result** indicates that the dissolved pollutant is detected at that location, while a **green result** indicates that the pollutant is not detected there.

4. Players can use the pattern of red and green sensor results to narrow down the possible pollution source. If a branch produces a green result, players can infer that additional sampling farther upstream along that branch may not be necessary. If a location produces a red result, players can continue testing farther upstream to determine where the pollution first enters the river.

5. Players can use the topographic map to examine the river network, identify upstream and downstream relationships, and view the locations and results of previously deployed sensors. This allows them to plan the next sensor placement strategically rather than sampling locations randomly.

6. Players can compare nearby sensor results to identify a transition from **green upstream** to **red downstream** along the same flow path. This pattern provides important evidence that the pollution source is likely located between those two sampling locations.

7. If players repeatedly deploy sensors in the same area or make placements that are unlikely to provide new information, DANI provides reminder dialogue encouraging them to avoid redundant sampling and choose locations that can more effectively narrow the search area.

8. Players can use the in-game chat tool to review previous conversations and DANI’s earlier guidance. This allows them to revisit sensor instructions, interpretive hints, or reminders about effective sampling strategies.

9. Players can progressively narrow the search area by combining sensor results, river-flow direction, and map information. A systematic strategy is to eliminate clean branches, continue tracing contaminated branches upstream, and focus new sensor placements near the boundary between clean and contaminated water.

10. Once the available sensor evidence sufficiently identifies the likely source area, DANI marks the pollution-source location as the major finding of the investigation.
