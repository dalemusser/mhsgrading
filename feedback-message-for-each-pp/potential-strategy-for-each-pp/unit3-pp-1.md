# Information and Strategies Available to Players

1. Tara provides narrative information through conversations that helps players understand the delivery task, including where her camp is located and how the crates need to be transported through the river system.

2. Players can review Dr. Toppo’s instructional video about water flow and river systems. The video provides relevant background knowledge for understanding how water moves through connected waterways and how objects placed in flowing water may travel downstream.

3. Players can use the in-game chat-history tool to review previous conversations with Tara and other characters. This allows them to revisit location clues or task instructions that may help them determine the appropriate delivery route.

4. Players can use the topographic map to identify Tara’s camp, examine the surrounding river network, and determine which river is hydrologically connected to the camp. Rather than selecting a river only because it is geographically close to the camp, players should consider the river’s connectivity and the direction of water flow.

5. Before releasing a crate, players can compare the crate’s current location, the structure of nearby river branches, and Tara’s camp location on the map. This can help them predict whether placing the crate into a particular river will allow the flowing water to carry it toward the camp.

6. Players can use the outcome of each crate delivery as additional evidence. A successful delivery confirms that the selected river provides an appropriate flow pathway toward Tara’s camp, while an unsuccessful delivery indicates that the selected river or branch does not lead to the intended destination.

7. Tara provides feedback about whether each delivery is successful. When a delivery is incorrect, her feedback can help players understand why the selected route did not work and reconsider the relationship between the river network, flow direction, and the location of the camp.

8. Players can use the combination of Tara’s clues, Dr. Toppo’s water-flow instruction, the topographic map, previous delivery outcomes, and Tara’s feedback to revise their route-selection strategy for the remaining crates.
